Who I Am

I'm a Senior Game & VR Developer with over 7 years of experience shipping games and immersive XR experiences across mobile, PC, and VR platforms.

Currently at Vinacts, I architect and build VR solutions for enterprise clients including Samsung and Hyundai — from custom VR interaction frameworks and architectural visualizations to an unannounced Steam title.

My background spans team leadership, systems architecture, custom editor tooling, shader programming, and performance optimization. I care about the craft: clean code, tight gameplay feel, and systems that scale.

When I'm not in Unity or Unreal, I'm prototyping mechanics, writing shaders, or pushing the edges of what real-time rendering can do.
